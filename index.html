Care Tips

Healthy water makes happy fish.

💧

Water Quality

Cycle the tank before adding fish. Test ammonia, nitrite and nitrate, and change 20–25% of the water weekly.

🌡️

Temperature

Use a heater and thermometer. Match the temperature to your species; tropical fish usually need 22–28°C.

🍤

Feeding

Feed small amounts once or twice a day, only what they finish in about 2 minutes. Overfeeding pollutes water.

🤝

Compatibility

Research temperament and adult size before buying, and avoid overstocking. Some fish, like male bettas, must live alone.
